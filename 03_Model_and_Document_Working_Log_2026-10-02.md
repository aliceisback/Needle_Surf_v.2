## 1. The hypothesis in the author's terms

### Core description

The proposed object is **one recurrent system / one particle**, described internally through inseparable `+` and `−` aspects. The author does not mean two ordinary independent particles, and has not equated the signs with charge, spin, matter/antimatter, or any already-established particle label.

The `+` and `−` aspects are understood as proceeding in one overall direction. The `−` aspect is described as arriving into the present interaction from a prior or “behind” stage of the same process. This is **not** a proposal that an object travels backward through time.

The important correction made by the author was:

> It does not travel backward; it comes into the present from a previous / behind moment of the process.

In the current wording, a sign is an internal relation to a reference moment or stage, not automatically a direction of travel in laboratory time.

### Indeterminacy and “brothering”

Before a functional relation is established, the possible outcomes are described as indeterminate. “Brothering” is the author's word for the creation or recognition of a strong, inseparable relation between aspects/nodes. In the author's description:

- a sensor or observer can be the practical need that makes the relation show itself;
- the linked aspects can later separate in appearance while retaining the significance of their bond;
- consciousness was discussed as a possible long-lived holder of such a relation, but this is conceptual language at this stage, not an established physical mechanism;
- the system is described as originating from one repeatedly recurring source/process.

This is a hypothesis vocabulary. It should not be silently replaced with “entanglement,” “measurement collapse,” “observer effect,” or another formal term unless a precise mathematical equivalence is later given.

### Method of discovery claimed by the author

The author says the model arose by tracing **similarities across different scales and processes**, where others may first separate phenomena by their differences. This is a legitimate way to generate hypotheses and analogies. It is not itself evidence that the analogous systems share one physical mechanism. A future paper should preserve both sides of that statement.

## 2. Time-language boundary

The following distinction must remain explicit in future drafts:

| Statement | Status in this work |
|---|---|
| A stage can be earlier than a chosen reference event (`Δt < 0` relative to `t0`) | Ordinary event-relative timing; potentially measurable |
| A preparation process can precede reported awareness or an external event | Established in some neuroscience tasks, with limited interpretation |
| An internal `−` aspect comes into the present from a prior/behind stage | Author's model description; requires operational definition |
| A physical object or causal signal travels backward in time | **Not claimed** |
| A usable message is sent faster than light | **Not claimed** |
| Reverse causation or communication into the past has been demonstrated | **Not claimed** |

For any experimental specification, laboratory clocks must still run forward. The proposal must identify a reference event `t0`, a preparation/selection setting `S`, and a measured relative time `Δt`. A negative `Δt` may only mean “earlier than `t0`”; it must not be used as a hidden assertion of reverse-time travel.

## 3. Conceptual collider / interaction proposal

The author suggested a high-energy interaction/collider thought experiment in which only the `−` aspect would be placed “inside” at a precisely chosen moment, while the `+` aspect remains outside as a reference. The proposal is motivated by the expectation that the `−` aspect is already present in, or becomes present within, the interaction/explosion.

This is a conceptual experimental direction, not yet a runnable protocol. It cannot be evaluated until the following are supplied:

1. **Physical identity:** What known particle, state, field mode, or detector-preparable system is meant by the `−` aspect?
2. **Preparation:** How is that state created, selected, transported, or injected without assuming the result?
3. **Reference:** What physically represents the retained `+` aspect, and how is its relationship to `−` measured?
4. **Timing:** What is the reference time `t0`; what timing precision and synchronization are needed; what does “the exact moment” mean numerically?
5. **Geometry:** What counts as “inside” and “outside” the interaction region?
6. **Observable:** Which detector-level quantity would differ from ordinary predictions—energy/momentum balance, correlation function, topology, angular distribution, multiplicity, or another pre-registered statistic?
7. **Control conditions:** What changes when `S` is altered, delayed, randomized, removed, or replaced by a matched conventional preparation?
8. **Statistics:** What is the null hypothesis, expected effect size, look-elsewhere protection, blinding plan, and independent replication path?

Without those definitions, “put only `−` inside” remains an important conceptual image but not yet a physically implementable instruction.

## 5. Nearby scientific context checked

The following works were added because they concern timing of awareness, intention, or speech. They are not evidence for the proposed particle model or collider effect.

### Subjective timing

- **Libet, Wright, Feinstein & Pearl (1979), “Subjective referral of the timing for a conscious sensory experience.”** The paper discusses delayed neural processing and a proposed subjective referral of experience to an earlier cortical time-marker. Its subject is the reported timing of conscious sensory experience. It does **not** show a physical entity moving backward in time.

### Readiness, decisions, and speech

- **Libet et al. (1983).** In a motor task, readiness-potential activity preceded participants' reported awareness of intending to move. This concerns motor preparation and retrospective timing reports, not a general proof about all thoughts or physics.
- **Soon et al. (2008).** In a constrained free-choice task, some choice-related brain information was decoded before reported awareness. The task and predictive strength are limited; it does not establish a universal predetermination of thought.
- **Carota et al. (2010), “Neural dynamics of the intention to speak.”** MEG activity associated with the intention to speak was observed before participants reported awareness of that intention. On a speech-centered graph, negative milliseconds mean “earlier than the chosen `t0` (such as speech onset or reported intention),” not travel into the past.
- **Castellucci et al. (2022).** Intracranial recordings identified speech-planning dynamics before speaking in interactive language use. This supports ordinary forward-time planning activity.
- **Schurger, Sitt & Dehaene (2012).** A stochastic accumulator account offers an alternative explanation of readiness-potential patterns. It is an important skeptical constraint: apparent early preparatory signals do not uniquely identify a settled decision or special temporal mechanism.

### Relation to the model

These works justify only a narrow statement: measurable neural preparation and subjective timing can precede a participant's reported present-awareness point. They do not validate the proposed `+`/`−` ontology, brothering mechanism, consciousness mechanism, collider intervention, gravity outcome, or nonlocal communication mechanism.

## 6. Established physics boundary kept in the document

The work used Bell-test/no-signalling results as a boundary, not as support for superluminal signalling. Bell-type experiments establish strong nonclassical correlations under their assumptions; they do not provide a controllable faster-than-light communication channel.

No claim was made that the proposed model has already:

- reproduced a Bell-test result;
- identified `+` or `−` with a standard physical quantum number;
- delivered a usable faster-than-light signal;
- demonstrated backward time travel, reverse causation, or messaging to the past;
- shown that consciousness creates or permanently maintains a physical relation;
- discovered a missing portion of gravity;
- produced a non-chaotic collider event;
- specified a currently buildable collider apparatus.

These omissions are not dismissals of the hypothesis. They are the difference between a generative model and a testable claim.

## 9. What would move the work forward

The next honest step is not to make the language grander. It is to choose one narrow, testable branch and write it so another researcher can try to disprove it.

A minimally testable version would contain:

1. one physical platform (not necessarily a collider at first);
2. a concrete definition of `+`, `−`, and the selection setting `S`;
3. one primary detector-level outcome;
4. a conventional null model and matched controls;
5. a predicted direction, scale, or decision rule set before looking at data;
6. a statement of what result would count against the model;
7. independent replication or at least an analysis route that an independent group could run.

New data that could change the current skeptical assessment would need to show a repeatable deviation from a well-matched ordinary model, with calibration, detector artifacts, selection bias, and post-hoc pattern finding ruled out. The evidence threshold becomes higher for a claim involving gravity, nonlocal correlations, or a fundamental change to causal interpretation.
