## Abstract

This work presents a preliminary exploration of quantum interactions through non-standard temporal relations. The hypothesis describes a single recurrent system with inseparable (+) and (−) aspects, investigated through conceptual collider thought experiments and temporal staging. The (+) aspect is retained as reference; the (−) aspect is proposed to enter interactions from a prior "behind" stage—not backward time travel, but an internal relational staging. Before functional relation is established ("brothering"), outcomes remain indeterminate. The research includes a complete reasoning trail, scientific boundary conditions, nearby neuroscience context (timing of awareness and intention), and remaining operationalization requirements. Candidate observable families are proposed for a high-energy interaction platform. This is exploratory research; the model has not been experimentally tested. Materials are provided for collaborative refinement and skeptical scientific analysis.

**Keywords:** quantum mechanics, temporal relations, particle interactions, consciousness and physics, experimental design, exploratory physics

## 1. Introduction and Core Hypothesis

### 1.1 Model Description

The proposed object is a **single recurrent system** described through inseparable `+` and `−` aspects. These are not independent particles or standard quantum numbers (charge, spin, matter/antimatter, etc.).

The essential claim is:

- One system exists with two inseparable internal aspects, `+` and `−`
- These aspects relate to a reference moment or stage, not automatically to direction of travel
- The `−` aspect is described as "arriving into the present from a prior/behind stage"
- Laboratory time runs forward; this is not a proposal for backward time travel
- Before a functional relation is established (called "brothering"), the possible outcomes remain indeterminate
- A sensor or observer need can trigger the practical appearance of the relation

### 1.2 Key Clarification: What This Model Does NOT Claim

The author explicitly states that this model has **not** demonstrated:

- Backward time travel or reverse causation
- Faster-than-light communication or signalling
- Consciousness as a fundamental physical mechanism
- Discovery of gravity's missing component
- A fully operationalized collider apparatus
- Any experimental result (this is exploratory work)
- Equivalence with "entanglement," "measurement collapse," or other standard terms

## 2. Temporal Relations and Scientific Context

### 2.1 Distinguishing Temporal Staging from Time Travel

A crucial distinction maintained throughout:

| Statement | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| A preparation event can occur earlier than a reference event `t0` | Standard timing; potentially measurable |
| Neural preparation precedes reported conscious awareness | Established in neuroscience (e.g., Libet, Soon) |
| An internal `−` aspect "comes from a behind stage" | Author's model description; requires definition |
| A physical object travels backward in time | **Not claimed** |
| Causation runs backward | **Not claimed** |
| A message is sent to the past | **Not claimed** |

For any experimental specification, laboratory clocks must still run forward. A negative `Δt` (relative to `t0`) means "earlier than the chosen reference," not time travel.

### 2.2 Neuroscience Boundary and Context

The following works were reviewed to understand timing of awareness, intention, and neural preparation:

1. **Libet, B., et al. (1979).** "Subjective referral of the timing for a conscious sensory experience." *Brain*, 102(1), 193–224.
   - Shows: Delayed neural processing; proposed subjective referral to earlier cortical time-marker
   - Does NOT show: Physical entity moving backward in time

2. **Libet, B., et al. (1983).** "Time of conscious intention to act in relation to onset of cerebral activity (readiness-potential)." *Brain*, 106(3), 623–642.
   - Shows: Readiness-potential precedes reported intention awareness (~350 ms)
   - Does NOT show: General proof that all thoughts are predetermined

3. **Soon, C. S., et al. (2008).** "Unconscious determinants of free decisions in the human brain." *Nature Neuroscience*, 11, 543–545.
   - Shows: Brain activity (decodable ~10 seconds before) correlates with later reported choice
   - Does NOT show: Decision determinism or loss of free will (limited task, strong predictive alternative explanations)

4. **Carota, A., et al. (2010).** "Neural dynamics of the intention to speak." *Cerebral Cortex*, 20(8), 1891–1897.
   - Shows: MEG activity associated with speech intention precedes subjective awareness
   - Does NOT show: Backward temporal flow (negative milliseconds = earlier than chosen `t0`, not time reversal)

5. **Castellucci, G. A., et al. (2022).** "A speech planning network for interactive language use." *Nature*, 602, 117–122.
   - Shows: Speech planning dynamics precede speaking
   - Context: Ordinary forward-time preparation in interactive language

6. **Schurger, A., Sitt, J. D., & Dehaene, S. (2012).** "An accumulator model for spontaneous neural activity prior to self-initiated movement." *PNAS*, 109(42), E2904–E2913.
   - Shows: Stochastic accumulator explains readiness-potential patterns without special mechanism
   - Implication: Apparent early signals don't uniquely identify settled decision or non-forward temporal process

**Relation to the model:** These results justify a narrow claim—measurable neural preparation precedes conscious awareness—but do not validate the `+`/`−` ontology, collider mechanism, or gravity outcomes.

## 3. Proposed Experimental Direction: Collider Thought Experiment

### 3.1 Conceptual Setup

The author proposes a high-energy interaction/collider thought experiment:

- Only the `−` aspect is placed "inside" the interaction region at a precisely chosen moment
- The `+` aspect remains outside as reference
- The interaction is expected to reveal properties of the `−` aspect that are already present or become present at that moment
- A preregistered observable should differ from ordinary models

### 3.2 What Remains to Be Specified

This is a conceptual direction, **not yet a runnable protocol**. Before evaluation, the following must be supplied:

| Required Element | Current Status |
|------------------|----------------|
| **Physical identity** | What known particle, state, or field mode represents `−` and `+`? |
| **Preparation method** | How are these states created, selected, transported? |
| **Reference definition** | What physically represents the retained `+` aspect? |
| **Timing specification** | What is `t0`? What precision? What does "exact moment" mean? |
| **Geometry** | What counts as "inside" and "outside" the interaction? |
| **Observable(s)** | Which detector-level quantities differ from prediction? |
| **Control conditions** | How does the observable change under parameter variation? |
| **Statistics** | Null hypothesis, effect size, look-elsewhere correction, blinding, replication? |

## 4. Candidate Outcome Families

The author proposed two candidate observables and intentionally withheld a third to preserve confidentiality.

### 4.1 Candidate 1: Possible Missing Gravitational Contribution

**Author's idea:** A small portion of what is missing from gravity could become visible under the proposed conditions.

**Experimental restatement:** A repeatable, control-resistant discrepancy in a predefined energy/momentum or detector observable, whose ordinary systematic explanations have been excluded.

**What it would NOT establish by itself:** Discovery of gravity's full missing component, a new force, or a complete gravity theory.

### 4.2 Candidate 2: Ordered Outgoing Pattern

**Author's idea:** The final particles may not exhibit fully chaotic output; a structured pattern may emerge.

**Experimental restatement:** A preregistered multiparticle correlation, topology, angular/multiplicity statistic, or entropy-like measure differing from the matched null model.

**What it would NOT establish by itself:** A special causal mechanism (unless standard production, detector, and analysis effects are ruled out).

### 4.3 Candidate 3: Intentionally Undisclosed

The author does not wish to state this outcome publicly at this time. It is preserved in the archive but not reconstructed or paraphrased without express authorization.

## 5. Methodology: Similarity-Based Reasoning as Hypothesis Generation

### 5.1 How the Model Arose

The author reports that the hypothesis emerged by tracing **similarities across different scales and processes**, where conventional science often first separates phenomena by their differences.

**Example:** The author observes structural analogies between particle pairing, molecular bonding, cellular division, and conscious relation—and proposes a unified staging mechanism.

### 5.2 Why This Is Valid (and Not Proof)

**Valid:** Analogical reasoning is a legitimate source of scientific hypotheses. Many major theories (gravity-as-curvature, thermodynamics-as-statistical-mechanics) arose from recognizing unexpected structural similarities.

**Not proof:** Observing that two systems share a mathematical structure does not establish that they share one underlying mechanism. Each analogous system requires independent empirical test.

## 6. What Would Move This Work Toward Testability

### 6.1 A Minimally Testable Version Would Require:

1. One physical platform (not necessarily a collider initially)
2. Concrete definition of `+`, `−`, and the selection setting `S`
3. One primary detector-level outcome
4. Conventional null model and matched controls
5. Predicted direction, scale, or decision rule set before looking at data
6. Clear statement of what result would count **against** the model
7. Independent replication or analysis route accessible to other groups

### 6.2 Evidence Threshold

New data overturning the current skeptical assessment would need to show:

- A repeatable deviation from well-matched ordinary predictions
- Calibration, detector artifacts, selection bias, and post-hoc pattern-finding ruled out
- For claims involving gravity, nonlocality, or causal reversal: higher bar still
