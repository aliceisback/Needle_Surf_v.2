---

### 2. **Preprint Servers**

**What it means:** Post your work to free academic preprint archives (no peer review needed; archives handle basic moderation).

**Best Platforms:**

| Platform | Focus | How to Use |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| **arXiv.org** | Physics, math, CS | Go to arxiv.org > Submit > Choose "General Physics" or "Quantum Physics" > Upload |
| **PhilSci-Archive** | Philosophy of Physics, Time | Go to philsci-archive.pitt.edu > Submit > Create account > Upload |
| **OSF Preprints** | Open Science | Go to osf.io/preprints > Upload > Versioning & DOI automatic |
| **EarthArXiv** | Physical sciences (broader) | Similar to arXiv, accepts wider topics |

**For arXiv specifically:**
1. Create an account (free, requires institutional email or endorsement)
2. Prepare a **PDF** of your work (combine files 03, 04, and 05 into one document)
3. Write a ~200-word abstract explaining the hypothesis and research status
4. Submit under "General Physics" or "Quantum Physics > Quantum Computing"
5. Include: "This is exploratory research, not a claim of verified discovery"
6. Get a preprint number (e.g., `2610.12345`)
7. Share the link: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12345`

**Advantages:**
- Established scientific recognition
- Automatic DOI for citations
- Indexed by Google Scholar
- Open to all researchers
- No cost

**Timeline:**
- arXiv reviews typically in 24–48 hours
- Other preprints often faster

### 3. **Institutional / Departmental Channels**

If you're affiliated with a university, research institute, or lab:
- Contact your institution's research office
- Ask about technical reports or working papers series
- Some institutions publish open-access collections
- Often includes library indexing and DOI

### 4. **Peer-Reviewed Journal (When Ready)**

**Not the immediate next step,** but important for later.

**When to consider:**
- After operationalization is complete (section 9 of file 03)
- Once you have experimental results or testable predictions
- When candidate outcomes are refined to measurable terms

**Good journals for this work:**
- **Physics journals:** *Physical Review Letters*, *Physics Letters B*, *Classical and Quantum Gravity*
- **Interdisciplinary:** *Frontiers in Physics*, *Scientific Reports*
- **Philosophy:** *Philosophy of Science*, *British Journal for the Philosophy of Science*
- **Neuroscience-adjacent:** *Neuroscience of Consciousness*, *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*

**Process:**
1. Refine your manuscript following journal guidelines
2. Include complete methods, controls, and negative results
3. Submit to Editor
4. Peer reviewers provide critique (2–8 weeks)
5. Revise based on feedback
6. If accepted, article is published online (weeks to months later)
