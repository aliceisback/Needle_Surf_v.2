## The Hypothesis (Brief Summary)

The proposed model describes a **single recurrent system** with inseparable `+` and `−` aspects:

- These are **not** independent particles or standard quantum numbers
- The `−` aspect is described as "coming into the present from a prior/behind stage"
- This is **not** backward time travel, but rather an internal staging relation
- Before functional relation is established, outcomes remain indeterminate
- "Brothering" (establishing relation) can be triggered by an observer/sensor need

**Key clarification:** The author does not claim this model has proven:
- Backward time travel or reverse causation
- Faster-than-light communication
- Consciousness as a fundamental physical mechanism
- Discovery of gravity's missing components
- A buildable collider apparatus (yet)

## Scientific Context Included

The research includes careful review of:
- **Neuroscience timing studies** (Libet, Soon, Carota, Castellucci, Schurger)
- **Bell-test/non-signalling results** — as boundary conditions, not support for FTL
- **Methodological notes** on similarity-based reasoning as hypothesis generation

None of these establish the model; they provide context for timing-related observations.

## What's Still Required for Experimental Testing

The model describes a conceptual collider thought experiment but lacks:

1. **Physical identity** — What known particle or state represents `−` and `+`?
2. **Preparation method** — How to create/select/inject these states?
3. **Reference condition** — How to measure the `+`/`−` relationship?
4. **Timing specification** — What is the reference time `t0`, with what precision?
5. **Geometry definition** — What counts as "inside" and "outside"?
6. **Observable(s)** — Which detector quantities would differ from ordinary predictions?
7. **Control conditions** — What changes under parameter variations?
8. **Statistics plan** — Null hypothesis, effect size, replication path?

## Candidate Outcomes

The author proposes two named candidates and intentionally withholds a third:

1. **Possible missing gravitational contribution** — A small portion of what's missing from gravity could become visible under the proposed conditions
2. **Ordered outgoing pattern** — Final particles may exhibit structured patterns rather than fully chaotic output
3. **Third candidate** — Intentionally undisclosed (preserved without inference or reconstruction)

These are **candidate observables**, not predicted discoveries.

## Archive Integrity

All files in this repository are preserved records. The principle of accuracy:
- User messages are verbatim where available
- Research results are recorded as performed, not reconstructed
- Scientific context is added with explicit limits noted
- Hidden reasoning, private system instructions, and access credentials are excluded
